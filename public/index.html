    How the check works

    1. Line up. MediaPipe finds both faces in your browser, then levels and scales them to the same size.
    2. Match the camera. Eyes, brows and lip edges do not change with skincare, so any loss of detail there is the camera. The sharper photo is softened until those parts match, and the eyes set exposure and colour.
    3. Measure twice. YouCam Skin Analysis scores the pair as published and again under the same light.

    Tested before launch

    Each test is a known answer: camera tricks with no skin change, a real skin change made with YouCam Skin Simulation, and an untouched copy of the same photo.

    Scores are added across six measures. Test faces are AI generated with YouCam.
